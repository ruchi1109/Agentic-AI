### Interview Preparation Guide for Ruchi Sharma - Data Scientist/ML Engineer

#### Technical Questions:
1. Can you explain a project where you developed an end-to-end solution for automated data validation in the pharmaceutical industry?
2. How did you utilize GenAI approaches like GANs and VAEs in your AI tool for synthetic data generation?
3. What was the impact of implementing the XGBoost ML model powered by a large language model in your project at GlaxoSmithKline?
4. Share details about your experience in identifying user intents in short texts using unsupervised learning techniques.

#### Behavioral Scenarios:
1. Describe a time when you had to lead a project with diverse data sources. How did you approach collating and analyzing the data?
2. How do you ensure data security compliance when working with Personally Identifiable Information (PII) in documents?
3. Talk about a challenging situation you faced during the development of a Power BI dashboard. How did you overcome it and deliver a successful project?

#### Company-Specific Questions:
1. What motivated you to join GlaxoSmithKline, and how has your experience been working in the pharmaceutical industry with a focus on data science?
2. How do you see your expertise in LLMs, GenAI, and data automation aligning with AlertMedia's focus on emergency communication solutions?
3. Can you discuss a project where you applied your knowledge of AWS services like EC2 and S3 in a real-world scenario?
4. How do you think your experience in developing production-grade ML models can contribute to AlertMedia's goal of improving employee safety through advanced technologies?

#### Strategic Talking Points:
1. Highlight your track record of delivering ML models that significantly reduce processing time while maintaining high quality standards.
2. Emphasize your expertise in practical, iterative development and experience with Python, R, cloud environments, and model deployment pipelines.
3. Showcase your proficiency in programming languages, ML/AI frameworks, LLM & GenAI tools, data analysis & manipulation, data engineering, cloud & deployment technologies, workflow & MLOps practices, and visualization communication tools.
4. Demonstrate your commitment to continuous learning and improvement through extracurricular activities like volunteering and cultural committee membership.

By preparing thoroughly with these interview questions and talking points, you can confidently showcase your skills, experience, and alignment with the job requirements to excel in your upcoming interviews.
